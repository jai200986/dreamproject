Helpful Travel Tips

Frequently Asked Questions

Essential tips to prepare for your memorable visit to [Destination Name].

When is the optimal time of year to visit [Destination Name]? +

The peak dry season runs from April to October, offering sunny blue skies, pleasant sea breezes, and minimal rainfall—ideal for surfing, diving, and hiking. The wet season (November to March) brings lush green foliage, quieter attractions, and attractive shoulder-season discounts.

Do I need a tourist visa upon arrival? +

Visitors from over 90 countries can obtain a 30-day Visa on Arrival (e-VoA or upon landing at Ngurah Rai International Airport) for approximately $35 USD, extendable for an additional 30 days. Please ensure your passport is valid for at least 6 months from entry.

Can I customize the existing travel packages? +

Absolutely! All packages are 100% modular. You can substitute activities (e.g., swap scuba diving for a culinary class), adjust trip durations, or upgrade your resort pool villas during the booking inquiry step.

What is the cancellation and refund policy? +

We offer free full cancellation up to 48 hours before your arrival date on all standard guided tours. Bespoke private villa reservations may vary slightly depending on hotel partner regulations.
